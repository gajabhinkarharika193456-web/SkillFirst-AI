# SkillFirst AI

## Problem

Many skilled individuals possess practical skills but may
lack formal degrees, certificates, resumes, or easy access
to suitable employment opportunities.

Their real-world abilities are often difficult to discover,
verify and match with relevant jobs.

## Solution

SkillFirst AI is a voice-first AI platform that identifies
a person's practical skills, assesses those skills using
AI-based questions, generates a skill score, matches the
worker with suitable jobs, and calculates recommended
remuneration based on the completed work.

## Core Idea

Don't ask what degree you have first.
Ask what skill you can actually perform.
